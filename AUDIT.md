# AUDIT — afergreens.com (2026-07-08)

Phase 1 audit of the current site on branch `redesign-2026`. Produced by a 7-dimension
multi-agent review (visual design, mobile, performance, accessibility, content/conversion,
AEO/SEO, product pages) with adversarial verification of every finding — 117 confirmed
findings (22 high, 57 medium, 38 low). Full list in the appendix; this summary is deduped
and prioritized.

## Executive summary

The site has a genuinely strong foundation: a deliberate design system (DESIGN.md),
good photography, working JSON-LD, llms.txt, robots.txt with AI crawlers, and per-product
pages with breadcrumbs and schema. But it falls short of a premium B2B instrument in four
systemic ways:

1. **Mobile is broken as a sales channel.** The stacked fixed header eats 20–27% of a
   phone screen, anchor links land content behind it, hero CTAs render below the fold on
   iPhone SE-class devices, and the quote form sits ~850px below the `#contacto` anchor.
2. **The conversion path fights the buyer.** The primary CTA is "Ver Cosecha" (browsing)
   while quote-request — the stated success metric — is the ghost button. Product pages
   have zero direct contact options, lose product context on the way to the form, and
   WhatsApp (the dominant Mexican B2B channel) appears once, at the very bottom.
3. **The content doesn't answer a buyer's qualifying questions.** No seasonality, MOQ,
   incoterms, pallet configs, lead times, or English version — despite US buyers being a
   primary audience and the copy itself promising "opciones de logística, MOQ y condiciones."
   Copy is systematically superlative ("Calidad Suprema", "más alta calidad") in direct
   violation of PRODUCT.md's own voice spec.
4. **Execution drifts from its own design system.** Amber gradient CTA strips, SCS green
   used decoratively, banned icon-card grids, six off-palette colors, ~2,300 lines of CSS
   duplicated across product pages with drift already present (nav order differs between
   home and product pages), and a cluster of WCAG contrast failures on the conversion form.

Measured page weight: index ~1.37MB fully scrolled (~500KB above fold); product pages
598–766KB. Images are 60–70% of it; a render-blocking 358KB icon library from unpkg serves
~12 icons.

## P1 — High-value improvements

**Conversion & content**
- WhatsApp as first-class channel: persistent button + pre-filled per-product message; product pages currently have zero contact options.
- Product CTAs lose context: link `/#contacto` with product preselection (`?producto=` + 3 lines of JS) or put a mini-form on product pages.
- Ficha técnica lacks what buyers qualify on: seasonality calendar, MOQ, pallet/container configs, downloadable spec sheet PDF.
- Voice pass: replace every superlative with a verifiable fact (SHU, calibre, cert numbers, mezquite process). Ticker omits Melón but includes "Calidad Suprema" twice.
- "100% Calidad Artesanal" badge → replace with a real number ("3× Primus GFS", year founded, hectares).
- Hero H1 says nothing a buyer needs ("El auténtico sabor del campo") — lead with what/where/proof.
- Form: phone AND essay required + no aviso de privacidad (LFPDPPP Arts. 15-17); FormSubmit captcha disabled on a raw-email endpoint (spam magnet).
- Footer is a dead end on all 5 pages: no product links, no contact repeat, no cert links, no privacy page.

**AEO / SEO**
- No FAQ content or FAQPage schema anywhere — the biggest quotable-answer gap (MOQ, seasons, what is chiltepin, SHU, how to verify Primus GFS).
- llms.txt has zero markdown links (violates llmstxt.org format) and contradicts the product pages (says "silvestre... a granel"; site says "cultivo controlado", 100g/1kg/10kg).
- `sameAs: []` — no Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, or any corroborating entity signal.
- NAP inconsistency: schema/footer say "La Cruz", visible content says "Saucillo".
- Offer `PriceSpecification` without price is invalid for rich results — use honest quote-based markup.
- Headings carry no entities ("Lo mejor del norte") — invisible to retrieval; add answer-style factual copy blocks.
- og:image is a 482KB 4:3 photo — WhatsApp link previews unreliable; export 1200×630 <200KB versions.

**Performance**
- Full-viewport compositing stack always on: grain overlay (`mix-blend-mode`) + blend-mode cursor + `backdrop-filter` header.
- Infinite rAF loop runs forever on every page, including mobile where the cursor is `display:none`.
- Header scroll animation animates `height`/`padding`/`backdrop-filter` (layout properties — banned by DESIGN.md itself).
- Fonts: ~181KB with unused weights requested; self-host subsets for ~90KB savings.

**Design-system integrity (violations of DESIGN.md's own rules)**
- Product-page CTA strip: full amber gradient surface (triple rule violation, off-palette `#E8830A`).
- SCS green used decoratively across product "Presentaciones" (Quarantine Rule).
- Ficha técnica = the exact banned icon+heading+text card grid.
- One Glow Rule: amber shadows on about-badge and pillar hover.
- Six off-palette colors introduced (spec gap: no light-surface amber token).
- 65ch Rule violated by every intro paragraph.
- Cert badges are the "clip-art certification badge" anti-reference both spec docs ban — use real Primus GFS/SCS marks.
- No favicon, apple-touch-icon, or theme-color on any page.

## Appendix — full verified findings (117)

### HIGH (22)

- **prefers-reduced-motion users get no mouse cursor at all (all 5 pages)** — `index.html:1315` _(accessibility)_
  - body has cursor:none on desktop (index.html:298) and relies on the JS-driven #cursor/#cursor-ring divs as the only pointer. The prefers-reduced-motion block hides those divs ('#cursor, #cursor-ring { display: none; }') but never restores body cursor:auto. Any desktop user with the OS 'reduce motion' setting enabled (the very users the media query exists to protect) is left with NO visible pointer anywhere on the site, making mouse navigation — and the quote form — nearly unusable. The same defect is on every product page (chiltepin/index.html:659-666, jalapeno, chipotle, melon). Same failure mode applies with JS disabled/failed: cursor:none is applied by CSS unconditionally on >768px viewports while the replacement cursor requires JS.
  - Fix: Add body { cursor: auto; } inside the prefers-reduced-motion block on all 5 pages. Better: gate the whole custom-cursor feature behind @media (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine) plus a JS-added class (e.g. html.js-cursor body { cursor:none }), so the native cursor is only hidden when the replacement is actually running.
- **Form and contact labels (stone on teal-deep) fail contrast at 3.75:1** — `index.html:1157` _(accessibility)_
  - All five quote-form field labels (.form-group label, index.html:1150-1159) and the four contact labels 'Ubicación / Ventas y Pedidos / WhatsApp / Correo Electrónico' (.contact-label, index.html:1112-1120) use --stone #9B9585 on the #07433A contact section. Computed 3.75:1 at 0.7rem (11.2px) uppercase — normal-size text requiring 4.5:1 under WCAG 1.4.3. These are the labels of the primary conversion form. This is spec-driven: DESIGN.md:171 assigns Stone to 'contact section h4 labels' on dark surfaces without a contrast check.
  - Fix: Switch these labels to --sage-mist #A8C4A9 (5.94:1 on teal-deep) or a lightened stone (#B8B2A2+). Amend DESIGN.md to restrict Stone to light surfaces.
- **SCS Green certification text fails contrast on light surfaces (2.75–2.92:1)** — `index.html:928` _(accessibility)_
  - In the Certificaciones section (bg --snow #F7F5F0), --scs-green #78A22F is used for: the section label '— Certificaciones' (index.html:928, 0.75rem), the headline em 'garantizada' (index.html:930 — 2.75:1 fails even the 3:1 large-text threshold), certificate numbers '#373629...' (.cert-num strong, index.html:1008-1012, 0.78rem), the 'Ver certificado' verification links (.cert-verify, index.html:1020-1037, 0.78rem — 2.92:1 on white), and 'SCS Global Services' (.certs-footer strong, index.html:1053). Product pages repeat it in .presentations (section-label/em on snow, chiltepin/index.html:442-444) and .pres-tag on white cards (chiltepin/index.html:494-502). This is the site's core trust content — certificate IDs and verify-links a buyer must read — and it is the least legible text on the page. DESIGN.md's SCS Quarantine Rule causes this by requiring the fixed PMS color for all certification references without a contrast provision.
  - Fix: Keep the SCS green for the badge fill (white-on-green icon circle passes) but render small text (cert numbers, verify links, labels) in a darkened variant (e.g. #567A1A ≈ 4.6:1 on white) or in teal-dark with a green underline/icon. Add a 'darkened for text' token to DESIGN.md's SCS rule.
- **Form submit result (success/error) is never announced to assistive technology** — `index.html:1732` _(accessibility)_
  - The AJAX submit handler toggles #form-error (index.html:1621) and #form-success (index.html:1623) via display:none/block and swaps the submit button's text. Neither container has aria-live, role='status'/'alert', and focus is not moved. On success the entire form (including the focused button) is display:none'd, silently dropping keyboard focus to <body>. A screen-reader user who submits a quote request gets no confirmation their request went through and no notification of failure — a direct conversion risk and a WCAG 4.1.3 (Status Messages, AA) failure.
  - Fix: Add role='status' (success) and role='alert' (error) — or aria-live='polite'/'assertive' — to the two message containers in the initial markup, and move focus to the success heading (tabindex='-1' + .focus()) after hiding the form.
- **Auto-scrolling ticker marquee has no pause/stop control (WCAG 2.2.2, Level A)** — `index.html:1368` _(accessibility)_
  - The amber ticker strip auto-starts, scrolls infinitely (animation: marquee 22s linear infinite, index.html:610), lasts longer than 5 seconds, and is presented in parallel with other content, but offers no mechanism to pause, stop, or hide it. It is only paused under prefers-reduced-motion (index.html:1313), which does not satisfy 2.2.2 — users with attention/cognitive disabilities who have not set that OS flag cannot stop the motion. aria-hidden correctly removes it from the accessibility tree but does not address the visual-motion requirement. This is a Level A failure on a site targeting WCAG 2.1 AA (PRODUCT.md:44).
  - Fix: Pause the animation on hover/focus and add a small accessible pause toggle, or pause it via animation-play-state when a button is activated. Alternatively make the strip static (it is decorative brand texture; a static band loses little).

- **Render-blocking 358KB icon library from unpkg CDN in <head> on every page — single point of failure for first paint** — `index.html:39 (same pattern: chiltepin/index.html:38, jalapeno/index.html:38, chipotle/index.html:38, melon/index.html:38)` _(performance)_
  - A synchronous, parser-blocking <script> to unpkg.com sits in <head> before any content. Measured: 357,796 bytes raw / 80,753 bytes gzip transfer. It ships the entire Lucide icon set to render ~12 icons (~6KB as inline SVG). Cost breakdown: (1) a serial DNS+TLS connection to a third origin with no preconnect hint, plus download and ~100-200ms parse/eval on mid-range mobile, all of it blocking first paint of every page; (2) unpkg is a community CDN with a documented history of multi-hour outages and latency spikes — if it stalls, every page on the site renders blank until the browser's network timeout, killing all conversions; (3) no SRI integrity attribute. The site's fonts get preconnect hints (index.html:34-35) but this render-blocking script gets none. Note the sync placement is currently doubling as CLS insurance — icons are swapped in by lucide.createIcons() at end of body before first paint; simply adding defer/async without fixing the markup would introduce icon pop-in layout shift (e.g. the .ph-meta icons above the fold in product-page heroes).

This removes 80KB of transfer, ~150ms of main-thread eval, one render-blocking third-party origin, the outage risk, and the icon-swap CLS hazard in one change. If a library must stay, self-host the file, add defer, and give every <i data-lucide> a fixed-size container.

Expected saving: ~500-600KB on index for mobile, ~150KB+ per product page.
