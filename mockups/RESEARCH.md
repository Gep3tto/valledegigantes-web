# Investigación de diseño high-end — briefs y hallazgos

Generado por investigación multi-agente (Awwwards, Typewolf, Codrops, marcas terroir). Los mockups A-D implementan estos briefs.

## Brief 1: LA CASA — ultra-editorial estate serif; the homepage as a wine-estate manifesto in five numbered chapters (Imperiale Bolgheri / Basilico grammar applied to chiles).
**concept**: AFER presented like a single-estate mezcal house: a defiant Spanish manifesto, numbered chapters (01 El Desierto, 02 La Cosecha, 03 El Humo, 04 Los Productos, 05 Exportación), oversized ghost folios, and data set as marginalia. Quiet, cream, type-led luxury — photography is rationed, never wallpapered. The reader should feel they are paging through the estate's private book, and that wholesale buyers are being let in on something.

**typography**: Three voices. Display: Fraunces variable (Google) at font-variation-settings 'opsz' 144, 'WONK' 1, 'SOFT' 0, weight 380–560, with TRUE italics for the mixed roman+italic device; used ONLY above 40px. Body/UI: General Sans (Fontshare) 400/500, body fixed at 1.0625rem/1.6 rem-scale (never vw). Data: DM Mono (Google) 400/500 exclusively for lot codes, SHU, coordinates, dates — 11–12px uppercase, letter-spacing .14em, font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums. Display scale rides viewport: H1 clamp(3.5rem,10.5vw,12.5rem), line-height .92, letter-spacing -0.035em; H2 clamp(2rem,6vw,6.5rem); eyebrows +.12em tracking with a 24px ::before rule. text-box-trim: trim-both on all headings so type butts flush against hairlines.

**palette**: Hard two-color commitment: --paper #EFE7D8 (desert bone) and --ink #1A0F0A (chile-black espresso). One accent, chile lacquer #A93B22, used ONLY on mono eyebrows, links and ::selection (<5% of pixels). No grays: all borders are color-mix(in srgb, currentColor 18%, transparent) hairlines. Sections alternate paper-on-ink / ink-on-paper via data-theme attributes; the fixed nav inverts as you cross (IO at rootMargin -50%).

**hero**: Type-first 95vh spread on paper, not a photo hero. Left 7 columns: the manifesto 'El norte no es <em>gentil</em>.' in Fraunces at clamp(3.5rem,10.5vw,12.5rem), the emotive word in true italic at 0.96em (the Lobos 1707 device); below it one DM Mono meta row: 'VALLE DE GIGANTES — 28.63°N 106.08°W — COSECHA 2025 — EXPORTACIÓN'. Right 4 columns: a single 4:5 storm photograph, graded, top edge flush with the H1 cap height (text-box-trim). On load: 500ms paper beat, then headline lines mask-reveal bottom-up (overflow:hidden wrappers, translateY(110%)→0, 120ms stagger, .9s cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)), then the photo curtains open via clip-path inset(0 0 100% 0)→inset(0) 1.1s while its img settles scale 1.18→1, meta row and nav fade-rise 24px last. One text link CTA ('Solicitar la hoja de cosecha →'), no buttons pair.

**layout_devices**: (1) Numbered ghost-folio chapters: each section carries an absolutely-positioned outlined counter — font-size clamp(6rem,18vw,14rem), color:transparent, -webkit-text-stroke 1px currentColor, opacity .18, z-index:-1 — plus hairline rule + mono eyebrow + poetic subtitle ('02 — La Cosecha / Se recoge a mano, uno por uno'). (2) Sticky split editorial spread: grid 5fr/7fr; left column position:sticky top:20vh holds chapter number, headline and a running mono caption that swaps via IO as the right column's stacked images (mixed 4:5 / 21:9 crops, one pulled -6vw into the gutter) scroll past. (3) Statement/dense pacing: a nearly-empty screen (one Fraunces sentence, 25vh margins) alternates with a dense spec screen (product table in DM Mono tabular-nums) — magazine-spread rhythm instead of uniform sections.

**motion**: One easing family site-wide: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1); reveals .7–.9s, hovers .3s. Scroll: a single shared IntersectionObserver ({rootMargin:'0px 0px -12% 0px', threshold:.15}) drives [data-reveal] variants — fade-rise 32px for text, clip-path curtains for images, per-line masks for headings, children staggered via --i * 80ms; fires once, never re-triggers. Progressive enhancement: @supports (animation-timeline: view()) adds an 8% parallax drift on chapter images (img scaled 1.15 inside overflow:hidden figures). Cross-document View Transitions (@view-transition{navigation:auto} + view-transition-name per product image) morph product photos into their detail pages. Hover system, applied uniformly: links use the gradient background-size underline that exits right and re-enters left; images scale 1→1.05 over .7s inside overflow:hidden and go from saturate(.85) to full color. Everything gated behind prefers-reduced-motion.

**photo_treatment**: Every image passes one grade: warm duotone via stacked pseudo-elements (.ph::before background #1A0F0A mix-blend-mode:lighten; .ph::after background #E8DCC8 mix-blend-mode:darken) so shadows go chile-black and highlights go bone — mixed-source photos read as one commissioned shoot. A fixed feTurbulence SVG grain layer at opacity .05, mix-blend-mode:overlay, sits over the whole page. Photos are rationed: never full-bleed except one 21:9 storm band between chapters 02 and 03; always non-default crops (4:5, 1:1 macro of chiltepin in hand); captions set in DM Mono sit ON the image edge, one rotated via writing-mode:vertical-rl.

**signature_moment**: The pronunciation seal: in the hero meta row, a small circled 'chil·te·PÍN ◉' mono button plays a 2-second audio pronunciation (Diaspora Co's expertise-tell), and while it plays the italic word in the manifesto pulses its font-variation weight 380→560→380 via CSS transition on font-variation-settings. Paired with the chapter-folio crossfade: a fixed ghost numeral at the right viewport edge counts 01→05 as you scroll (scroll-spied by the section IO), so the whole homepage reads as a paged book.

## Brief 2: RÓTULO — kinetic-type poster maximalism; Mexican sign-painter energy at export scale, La Revoltosa's red/cream duotone with velocity-reactive marquees.
**concept**: The loud direction: product names AS the design. A hard red/cream duotone, viewport-cropped display caps, marquee dividers that skew with scroll velocity, and cut-out harvest photography pasted over giant type like labels on a crate. It reads mercado-poster on the surface but exporter underneath — every giant word is annotated with mono spec data (SHU, lot, harvest). High energy, zero icons, zero cards.

**typography**: Display: Clash Display (Fontshare) 600/700, uppercase only, set at poster scale — hero words 14–18vw (clamp(4rem,16vw,15rem)), line-height .85, letter-spacing -0.02em, manually broken per line. Body: Satoshi (Fontshare) 400/500/700 at 1.0625rem/1.55. Data: Space Mono (Google) 400/700 for every number and label — 11px uppercase +.16em tracking, tabular-nums for the spec strips. Ratio discipline: display-to-body ≥ 12:1. Mixed-case is banned in display; Satoshi handles anything that must be read at length.

**palette**: Duotone commitment in the La Revoltosa/sakazuki family: --rojo #C8371F (chile lacquer red) and --crema #F3EDE2 (masa cream), plus --tinta #161008 permitted only for small body text on crema. Sections are either rojo-field with crema type or crema-field with tinta type — never a third scheme. ::selection is crema-on-rojo; scrollbar-color tinta/crema; focus-visible 2px solid rojo offset 3px.

**hero**: 100dvh solid rojo field. Three stacked lines of Clash Display crema caps — 'CHILTEPÍN / CHIPOTLE / MELÓN' — each at ~16vw, deliberately cropped: line 1 bleeds off the left viewport edge, line 3 off the right (negative margin-inline, overflow hidden on body sections not on the page). The chiltepin-in-hand photo, cut out and red-duotoned, is absolutely positioned overlapping lines 1–2 with a 1px crema outline like a pasted label. Under each word, a Space Mono annotation: 'SHU 50,000–100,000 · SILVESTRE · LOTE 04'. On load: the three lines slide in alternating from left/right (translateX(±8vw)+opacity, .9s cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), 140ms stagger), the photo drops in last with a 2° rotation settling to 0.6°, then a thin marquee strip at the viewport bottom starts rolling 'EXPORTADOR · DESIERTO CHIHUAHUENSE · COSECHA 2025 ·'. Nav is Space Mono caps pinned top, no logo block — the wordmark AFER sits stamped top-left at 45° -2°.

**layout_devices**: (1) Marquee dividers between every section: duplicated flex track, width:max-content, @keyframes to translateX(-50%), 50–80s linear infinite, content in 8–12vw Clash caps ('MEZQUITE — HUMO — 72 HORAS —'); aria-hidden, paused/static under prefers-reduced-motion. (2) The product index as giant text rows: full-width rows of 9vw Clash caps rendered in outline (-webkit-text-stroke 1px, fill transparent) that flood solid crema on hover while a lerped, cursor-following 4:5 harvest photo (position:fixed figure, pos+=(target-pos)*.1 in one rAF loop, slight rotation from x-velocity) chases the pointer and swaps per row — the Codrops menu-image pattern. (3) Spec-strip crate labels instead of cards: each product block is a bordered 1px crema rectangle with corner registration marks (::before/::after), pipe-delimited Space Mono data line, and the price/format ('ARPILLA 30 KG') as the biggest element — B2B data treated as poster copy.

**motion**: Load: alternating slide-ins as above, one easing token cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1). Scroll: velocity-reactive marquees — a rAF loop reads scroll delta, lerps a --skew custom property clamped to ±6deg and a speed multiplier applied to every marquee track, so fast scrolling shears the type like wet paint. Section reveals are hard cuts, not fades: clip-path inset wipes (inset(0 0 100% 0)→0, .8s) on rojo/crema blocks so color fields snap like screen-printed layers. Hover: text rows flood-fill via -webkit-text-stroke→color swap + the cursor-chasing image; the single CTA ('PEDIR MUESTRAS') is magnetic — mousemove offsets translate at 0.3x from center, label at 0.45x, lerped in rAF, gated to (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine). All motion off under prefers-reduced-motion; marquees become static centered lines.

**photo_treatment**: Photos are never rectangles-in-a-grid: they are cut-outs and duotones treated like paste-ups. Two recipes only: (a) red duotone — grayscale(1) then ::before #C8371F mix-blend-mode:lighten + ::after #F3EDE2 mix-blend-mode:darken; (b) full cut-out product/hand shots (pre-cut WebP with transparency) sitting directly on the color field with a hairline crema outline and a tiny mono caption underneath like a museum label. Every image gets 1–2° of rotation (rotate(1.4deg)) and 6% grain. No photo is ever wider than 45vw — type stays the biggest thing on every screen.

**signature_moment**: The scroll-shear: grab the page and throw it, and every marquee and outlined headline shears up to 6° with your velocity, snapping back with a lerped overshoot when you stop — the whole page feels screen-printed on rubber. Combined with the product-index rows where 'CHILTEPÍN' floods from outline to solid as a red-duotoned harvest photo chases your cursor across it. No other exporter site in this category moves like this.

## Brief 3: MONZÓN — pinned cinematic scroll-film; the desert monsoon as a dark documentary told in sticky 100dvh chapters with a lightning-flash transition.
**concept**: The homepage is a short film about the storm that makes the chile: near-black, full-bleed storm photography pinned under scrolling title cards, condensed trailer caps, mono timecodes, and a Kalso-style persistent chapter rail. Sparse, heavy, slow. 'El norte no es gentil' is the opening title card, kept in Spanish. Buyers scroll through EL DESIERTO → LA TORMENTA → LA COSECHA → EL HUMO → EL EMBARQUE and land on a spec-sheet finale — story-first, commerce as the credits.

**typography**: Display: Archivo variable (Google) — width axis at 125 (Expanded), weight 700–800, uppercase, for chapter title cards: clamp(2.8rem,9vw,10rem), line-height .9, letter-spacing -0.01em (trailer-card voice, Roc Grotesk Compressed territory). Editorial passages: Sentient (Fontshare) 300/400 + italic at 1.25rem/1.7 for the narrated paragraphs between chapters. Data/timecode: JetBrains Mono (Google) 400/500 — 11px caps +.14em for coordinates, timecodes ('00:02 / LA TORMENTA'), SHU and lot data, tabular-nums. Small labels are the only positive-tracked text; body tracking 0.

**palette**: Night grade: --noche #12100E (warm storm black) as the base field and --hueso #E4D9C4 (bone) for all type; one accent, ember red #B3402A, appears exactly twice — the final CTA and the live 'TEMPORADA ABIERTA' status dot. Backgrounds never leave #12100E except the finale spec section, which inverts to hueso-on-noche→noche-on-hueso as dawn after the storm. Styled dark scrollbar (scrollbar-color #3A2F26 #12100E), hueso-on-ember ::selection, theme-color #12100E.

**hero**: A 300vh pinned intro: wrapper min-height:300vh, child position:sticky top:0 height:100dvh overflow:hidden. Inside, three stacked stills — storm-over-valley, valley floor, chiltepin-in-hand — crossfade by opacity as scroll progress crosses 0.33/0.66 (sentinel divs + one IntersectionObserver toggling data-step, CSS transitions 1.2s). Over them: JetBrains Mono slate top-left ('28.63°N 106.08°W — DESIERTO CHIHUAHUENSE — MONZÓN 2025') and the title 'EL NORTE / NO ES / GENTIL' in Archivo Expanded caps, each line mask-revealing as its still arrives. Page load: a once-per-session preloader — noche field, wordmark, mono counter 0–100 keyed to hero image decode(), exiting via clip-path inset(0 0 100% 0) .8s — then the first still scales 1.15→1 over 1.2s and the first title line rises. A thin hueso progress rail with five chapter ticks sits fixed at the right edge from the first frame.

**layout_devices**: (1) Sticky-stage chapters: every chapter is a 250–400vh wrapper with a sticky 100dvh stage; a rAF-throttled handler writes progress to a --p custom property that children key transforms/opacity off — text panels swap while the pinned photograph slowly scales 1→1.08 and darkens. (2) The persistent chapter rail (Kalso device): fixed right-edge vertical rail with mono labels 01–05 that scroll-spy the active chapter (IO at rootMargin -50%), clickable, with a scaleY progress thread driven by animation-timeline: scroll() where supported. (3) Credits-style data blocks: between chapters, full-width mono tables on noche — 'AHUMADO: MEZQUITE, 72 H / SECADO: AL SOL / HUMEDAD: <12%' — set like film credits, two columns, hairlines at currentColor 15%.

**motion**: Slow and heavy: large moves 1.0–1.2s, all on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1); nothing bounces. Scroll is the scrub: stills crossfade and captions swap only from scroll position, implemented with position:sticky + IO steps (works everywhere) and upgraded via @supports (animation-timeline: view()) to compositor-driven parallax drift (translateY -8%→8% on scaled stills). Optional Lenis (~4KB, vendored, lerp .09) to weight the scroll since transforms are scroll-keyed — destroyed under prefers-reduced-motion, where all chapters collapse to static full-bleed images with visible text. Title cards use the line-mask reveal (translateY(110%)→0, 150ms stagger). Hover is minimal by design: mono links underline via background-size 1px wipe; the two ember CTAs brighten brightness(1.15) over .3s. View Transitions fade root between pages (fade-out .25s / fade-in .35s).

**photo_treatment**: One cinematic grade: crushed warm blacks and letterboxing. Images sit in a noche-filled wrapper with img{mix-blend-mode:luminosity} so the photograph inherits the page's warm black, then filter:contrast(1.08) brightness(.92); highlights read bone, never white. Full-bleed 100dvh or letterboxed 21:9 bands only — no small inset photos until the finale spec section, where products appear as tight 1:1 macro crops on hueso. Animated grain: the feTurbulence overlay at opacity .07 with an 8-steps/s background-position jitter (steps() keyframes), frozen under prefers-reduced-motion.

**signature_moment**: The lightning cut: at the transition from LA TORMENTA to LA COSECHA, an IO boundary fires a once-only .flash class — a 240ms steps(3) keyframe flickers the pinned storm still through filter:invert(1) brightness(1.6) and back, with the chapter title stamping in during the afterimage. It reads as a real lightning strike lighting the valley, costs ~6 lines of CSS, and is fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion (replaced by a plain crossfade). Nobody forgets the site where the storm actually strikes.

## Brief 4: MANIFIESTO — swiss-minimal trade gallery; the homepage as a beautiful cargo manifest, Nordic Approach discipline where the availability table IS the hero.
**concept**: The pure-B2B flex: strip everything to an exposed hairline grid, one grotesk, one mono, and let real trade data carry the luxury — lots, harvest dates, SHU ranges, availability status, port terms. It looks like the world's most beautifully typeset export document (manifiesto de carga doubles as manifesto). Photography is small, precise, and earned. This is the direction that says 'we are the serious counterparty' to a procurement director in Rotterdam or Tokyo.

**typography**: Two voices only, no serif anywhere. Primary: Switzer (Fontshare) — 400/500/600 for everything from 11px UI to the 9vw statement (H1 clamp(2.75rem,9vw,10rem), line-height .95, letter-spacing -0.04em; Söhne/Suisse register). Data: DM Mono (Google) 400/500 for the entire manifest layer — table figures, lot codes, dates, coordinates — always font-variant-numeric: lining-nums tabular-nums, 12px, +.08em tracking in caps rows. Scale contrast does the luxury work: 9vw statement over 12px mono rows, nothing in between except 1.0625rem body. text-box-trim on the H1 so it sits flush on the grid's top hairline.

**palette**: Gallery paper #FBF9F4 and warm ink #191510 — near-white and near-black WITH hue, never pure. Hairlines everywhere at color-mix(in srgb, currentColor 15%, transparent). One functional accent: chile red #BE3A21 used exclusively as data semantics — the 'DISPONIBLE' status dot, the active row marker, ::selection, and the stamp (below); under 3% of pixels. One inverted ink section (the origin band) breaks the whiteness exactly once.

**hero**: The manifest-as-hero. First viewport: exposed 12-column grid with visible vertical hairlines; a top mono strip reads 'AFER GREENS — VALLE DE GIGANTES — EXPORTADOR — CHIHUAHUA, MX — 28.63°N 106.08°W'. Below, the statement in Switzer 600: 'Chiles secos de origen único. Del desierto al puerto.' at 9vw. Directly under it — still in viewport one — the live availability table: five DM Mono rows (PRODUCTO / LOTE / COSECHA / SHU / PRESENTACIÓN / ESTADO), e.g. 'CHILTEPÍN SILVESTRE · L-04 · 2025 · 50–100K · 5 KG · ● DISPONIBLE'. On load: vertical hairlines draw in via scaleY(0)→1 transform-origin:top (.7s, 40ms stagger), the statement's two lines mask-reveal, then table rows cascade opacity+translateY(16px) at 60ms stagger, status dots blinking on last. Wholesale is a first-class top-nav item ('Mayoreo / Export'), not a footer link.

**layout_devices**: (1) The exposed working grid: persistent 1px column rules that content visibly hangs from; elements deliberately cross them (a 21:9 valley photo spanning columns 2–11, its mono caption sitting in column 1) so the grid reads intentional, not template. (2) Spec-sheet product blocks in place of cards: one product per full-width row — cut-out photo on paper spanning 3 columns, origin-as-title ('Chiltepín Silvestre — Sierra, frontera Sonora–Chihuahua — Cosecha 2025') in Switzer 500, and a pipe-delimited mono spec line ('SHU 50,000–100,000 | SECADO AL SOL | HUMEDAD <12% | LOTE 04'); rows separated by hairlines only, no boxes, no shadows, no radius. (3) The harvest calendar band: a horizontal 12-month scroll-snap strip (overflow-x:auto inside its own container) marking each product's harvest window in mono — Nordic Approach's harvest calendar as a designed object.

**motion**: Precision over spectacle: one easing cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), reveals .6s, hovers .25s, entrance distances ≤16px — motion so restrained it reads as machinery. A single IO drives hairline draws and row cascades, once only. @supports (animation-timeline: scroll()) powers a 1px ink progress line under the fixed header (scaleX) and a header that tightens padding over animation-range: 0 120px. Hover craft: table rows tint background to color-mix(ink 4%) while a 4:5 graded field photo fades in pinned to the row's right edge (opacity 0→1, scale .98→1, .35s) — data first, image as verification; links underline-wipe; count-up numerals (IO + rAF, eased, tabular-nums) animate the credibility stats ('26 TONELADAS / TEMPORADA', '3 CONTINENTES'). View Transitions morph the row's thumbnail into the product page hero via matching view-transition-name. Reduced motion: everything static, dots don't blink.

**photo_treatment**: Photography is evidence, not atmosphere: small, consistently graded (single neutral-warm curve — grayscale(1) sepia(.28) saturate(1.3) contrast(1.05) tuned once), clipped precisely to grid columns, mostly 4:5 and 1:1 macro. Exactly two large images on the whole page: the 21:9 valley band crossing the grid, and the inverted ink origin section where the storm photo runs mix-blend-mode:luminosity into #191510. Every image carries a mono caption with a real fact ('Recolección manual, 1,240 msnm'). Grain at .04 opacity over the ink section only.

**signature_moment**: The customs stamp: when the availability table finishes its load cascade, a circular 'REVISADO · AFER GREENS · JUL 2026' stamp in #BE3A21 rotates in at -8° on the table's corner (clip-path circle reveal + 1.05→1 settle, 500ms) — and the same stamp animation fires as the confirmation state of the 'Solicitar hoja de precios' form. The whole site behaves like a document being processed by a very good customs office; the stamp is the payoff no template would ever ship.
